# Preserve the source logo

The master is `assets/luka-logo-full.svg`. It contains the arch, wordmark and descriptor as vector paths. Transparent PNG files are exports of that artwork.

An image reference guides a generative model. It does not guarantee that the model preserves the logo's contours, spacing or proportions.

For final production:

1. Generate the scene with a clear, unprinted placement area.
2. Place the approved artwork through a controlled mockup or compositing step.
3. Match perspective and surface curvature, preserving the lockup's internal geometry.
4. Check legibility, clear space, colour and material integration at delivery size.
5. Have a person approve the finished asset.

Use source overlays to inspect generated artwork. A source-derived logo still needs a believable material treatment; exact artwork alone does not make a convincing mockup.
